Message from the President

Welcome to our foundation's website, dedicated to supporting children, young girls, and women in underserved areas of Côte d'Ivoire.

It's an honor to welcome you on behalf of our whole team. Together, we form a passionate, committed community, united by one noble cause: bringing joy and hope to those who need it most.

The path toward healing, independence, or resilience can be full of obstacles — but we believe deeply in the power of unity and solidarity to overcome them. We're endlessly grateful to our valued partners, whose generosity and steady support are the pillars of our work.

Thanks to your donations and commitment, we can offer a safe home, access to vital care, quality education — and above all, sow happiness and hope in lives often marked by adversity.

Every contribution, large or small, has a real impact on the well-being and future of the people we support. Join us in this mission for a more inclusive, more just world.

Coulibaly Mariam épouse Ouattara
President of the FAMO Foundation
