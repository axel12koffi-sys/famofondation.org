Our achievements

Every achievement reflects our commitment to transforming lives and building a better future for rural West African communities, thanks to the support of our donors, partners, and teams.

Education: opening doors

Building and equipping schools, supplying school materials, training teachers, and tailored programs: these efforts have driven up enrollment and given thousands of children the prospect of a brighter future.

Health: saving lives

Vaccination campaigns, free medical consultations, hygiene and nutrition awareness: these actions have helped reduce infectious disease and improve maternal and child health.

Vocational training: building futures

In partnership with local businesses and institutions, our carpentry, sewing, farming, and computing courses have let many young people and adults gain practical skills, find work, or start their own business.

Support for the needy: a safety net

Distribution of food, clothing, and essential goods, emergency relief programs: we've brought immediate relief to the most vulnerable and helped families weather crises.

Impact that changes lives

Children smiling on their way to school, healthier families, young entrepreneurs thriving: these stories prove our mission makes a real difference. These achievements are yours too — thank you for your ongoing generosity and commitment.

One action, one life

Every gift, big or small, supports our education, health, and solidarity programs for families in rural Côte d'Ivoire.
