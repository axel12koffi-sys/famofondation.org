Vocational training

Vocational training is a key driver of economic development and individual independence.

Practical, real-world skills

Our program offers hands-on learning opportunities across a range of trades: carpentry, sewing, farming, and information technology. We partner with local businesses and training centers to offer courses aligned with the needs of the job market.

Many young people and adults gain valuable skills this way to find stable work or start their own business. Personal development workshops build confidence and life skills essential to professional success.

A driver for communities

By supporting vocational training, we help create jobs and drive local economic development. Anyone equipped with the right means and skills can help their community thrive.
