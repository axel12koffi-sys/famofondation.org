The FAMO Foundation

A non-profit founded in 2023, FAMO works alongside local leaders to give vulnerable populations — first and foremost women and children — the means to build a better future.

  • Sustainable projects, grounded in the real needs of communities
  • A focus on high-impact education and essential healthcare
  • A conviction: collective action transforms lives
Learn more

Our programs

Four complementary areas of action for lasting impact.

Education

Building and equipping schools, training teachers, and opening up new horizons for children in rural areas.

Learn more

Vocational training

Practical skills — carpentry, sewing, farming, computing — for economic independence.

Learn more

Health

Free consultations, vaccination campaigns, and prevention for the health of mothers and children.

Support for the needy

A safety net for the most vulnerable: food, essential goods, and emergency relief.
