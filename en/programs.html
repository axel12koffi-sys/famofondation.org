Our programs

Four complementary areas of action, designed to meet the real needs of rural communities in Côte d'Ivoire and create lasting impact.

Education

Education is the cornerstone of development. We build and equip schools, train teachers, and provide essential learning resources.

Discover the program

Vocational training

Carpentry, sewing, farming, computing: practical, job-market-aligned learning for economic independence.

Discover the program

Health

Health is a basic right. Free consultations, vaccination, maternal and child health, hygiene and nutrition.

Discover the program

Support for the needy

Humanitarian aid and social support for families facing hardship: food, essential goods, emergency relief.

Discover the program
