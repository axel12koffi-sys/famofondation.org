Who we are

Founded in 2023, the FAMO Foundation is a humanitarian organization serving vulnerable populations in Côte d'Ivoire. Driven by philanthropy and compassion, we work to reduce social inequality through health and education.

Our story

What began as a simple idea quickly became a mission, then a reality. Since 2023, we've learned, grown, and evolved — but our commitment to rural West African communities has stayed constant.

Our team

Professionals in education, health, community development, and non-profit management bring their skills and passion together in service of a shared cause.

Our values

Commitment, solidarity, respect, transparency, and innovation guide every action and decision we make — a daily reminder of why and how we work.

Our community

Generous donors, committed partners, and dedicated volunteers: it's thanks to their trust that we can keep changing the lives of people in rural areas.

Our commitment

Everyone deserves a dignified, fulfilling life. We're determined to work tirelessly to make that vision real.

One action, one life

Every gift, big or small, supports our education, health, and solidarity programs for families in rural Côte d'Ivoire.
